Tasks Tracker

A personal work-planning app for tracking planned work, random to-dos, and weekly schedules — with optional two-way sync to Google Drive and Google Calendar.

Tasks Tracker helps plan upcoming work items, keep a running list of miscellaneous to-dos, and see everything laid out on a weekly plan. When signed in with a Google account, it can back up your data to a single file in your own Google Drive and optionally create matching events on your Google Calendar for anything you schedule.

This is a small, personal tool built and used by its developer and a limited number of invited users — it is not a public product or commercial service.
