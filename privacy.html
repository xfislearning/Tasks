Privacy Policy — Tasks Tracker

Last updated: September 2026

Tasks Tracker is a personal work-planning tool. This page explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and how it is stored.

Who operates this app

Tasks Tracker is operated by Xiaofang "Sophie" Yang. Questions can be sent to qentro.startup@gmail.com.

What data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, Tasks Tracker requests the following permissions:

  • Basic profile and email — used only to identify you inside the app (your name and email are shown in the app's header).
  • Google Drive (drive.file scope) — used to save and load a single backup file containing your task data, so it can sync across your own devices. The app can only see and edit files it creates itself; it cannot browse or access any other files in your Drive.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.events scope) — used to create, update, and delete calendar events that correspond to work items you explicitly schedule inside the app (for example, a "Work Plan Ahead" item with a start date). This lets scheduled items appear on your Google Calendar and stay in sync when you edit or delete them in the app.

How your data is used and stored

All task data is stored locally in your browser and, if you choose to sign in, backed up to a single file in your own Google Drive account. Calendar events created by the app live in your own Google Calendar. Tasks Tracker does not have its own server or database — it does not transmit, sell, or share your data with any third party, advertiser, or analytics service. No one other than you (the signed-in user) can access your Drive backup file or the calendar events the app creates on your behalf.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete your data at any time by removing the backup file from your Google Drive, deleting the synced events from your Google Calendar, or clearing your browser's local storage for this site. You can also revoke the app's access entirely at Google Account permissions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "last updated" date.
